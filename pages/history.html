THE HISTORY OF SIGNORI

WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

EST. 2014

Founded in 2014 by Kris and Nenad, SIGNORI began with a vision to create suits shaped around the individuality of each client. Built on precision, craftsmanship and timeless elegance, that vision has grown into a distinctive expression of modern luxury.

THE ART OF BESPOKE LIVING


It started with a simple need: to create a suit that truly felt right. Kris Popovski and Nenad Pantovic began by creating one for themselves — built around their own fit, style and standards. What started as a personal pursuit soon revealed a wider demand, leading to the creation of SIGNORI and a new approach to bespoke tailoring in Macedonia.


First Creations

The beginning of the Signori story. Our first creations, shaped by craftsmanship and a timeless vision.

THE NEXT CHAPTER IN FABRIC

A new chapter begins with the finest materials. In collaboration with Scabal, Loro Piana, Zegna, Signori explores exceptional fabrics that will define the next generation of our collections.

A SIGNATURE OF CRAFT

Every detail begins with the right material. Carefully selected Scabal fabrics meet the distinctive identity of Signori, creating the foundation for pieces defined by refinement, character and timeless craftsmanship.

REFINED CRAFTSMANSHIP

True luxury resides in details invisible to the untrained eye. From hand-selected fabrics to meticulous fitting sessions, perfection remains our ultimate benchmark.

OUR CONTINUING LEGACY

As time evolves, our dedication to bespoke perfection remains unchanged. We continue to shape the future of tailoring, creating timeless garments for discerning gentlemen across generations.
