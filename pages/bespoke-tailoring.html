Bespoke Tailoring Process

A SIGNORI garment is created through a process shaped entirely around the individual. From the first conversation to the final fitting, every stage is considered with intention and precision. Measurements, materials, construction and finishing come together to create a garment that feels distinctly yours. This is where craftsmanship becomes personal.

The Consultation

The bespoke journey begins with a personal consultation. We take the time to understand your style, lifestyle, preferences and the occasion for which the garment is being created. This conversation allows our tailor to understand what you want to express through your clothing. Together, we establish the direction for your SIGNORI piece.

Measurement

Precision begins with understanding your proportions. Every measurement is carefully taken and considered. Your pattern is created specifically for you.

Design

Your vision takes shape through fabric, cut and detail. Every element is carefully selected, from the silhouette to the smallest finishing touch. Nothing is left to chance.

Craft

The garment is brought to life by skilled hands. Each piece is carefully cut, constructed and finished with precision. Craftsmanship defines every stage.

Fitting & Finishing

The final fitting brings everything into balance. Subtle adjustments refine the fit, comfort and silhouette. Every detail is perfected before completion.
