A SIGNORI Bespoke Suit is created specifically for you, from the initial consultation and measurements to the final fitting. Every detail is designed around your body, preferences, lifestyle and personal style.

Your experience begins with a personal consultation where we discuss your needs, preferences and the occasion for which the garment is being created. This is followed by precise measurements and posture analysis to establish your individual blueprint.

More than 30 precise measurements are taken, together with posture analysis, to create a pattern specifically for your body. This allows the garment to achieve a precise and natural fit.

Intermediate fittings are part of the bespoke process. They allow our tailors to refine the fit, drape and silhouette down to the smallest detail before the garment is completed.
