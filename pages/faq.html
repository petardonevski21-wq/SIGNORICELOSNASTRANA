A SIGNORI bespoke suit is an individually drafted garment constructed completely from scratch according to your exact anatomical specifications, styled personally to your tastes, and hand-stitched by our master artisans.

The journey begins with an initial consultation in our atelier, where we discuss your preferences, lifestyle needs, preferred silhouettes, and review our library of luxury European fabrics.

Over 30 precise anatomical measurements and posture evaluations are recorded by our master tailor to construct your unique paper pattern archive.

Typically, 2 to 3 personal fittings are conducted to refine balance, drape, and precision tailoring before final hand-finishing.

Absolutely. Every single aspect—from cloth composition, lining silk, lapel width, horn buttons, to shoulder padding structure—is completely custom.

The full creation process requires approximately 4 to 6 weeks of meticulous artisan work from pattern cut to final delivery.

Your bespoke paper pattern is securely preserved in our archive custody, allowing effortless future commissions without requiring repeat measurement sessions.

Unlike mass production or made-to-measure modifications, true bespoke crafts an uncompromised garment unique to your physical structure with full floating canvas interiors and hand-sewn detail.
